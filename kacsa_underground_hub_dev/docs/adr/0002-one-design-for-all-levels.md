# One design for all levels: the hall of the game station

Every level, rail or tram, uses the hall models of the game's underground tram station, with the platform strips and the cargo depot of the source mod. There are no custom rail models, no Japanese style and no track bed. A rail level keeps real railway edges across the hall, at the depth of a tram level. The owner wants the look of Simple Underground Transfer Station and no design options. The cost is a rail tunnel that joins a hall of a tram profile, and a rail platform that is lower than a custom rail hall would give.

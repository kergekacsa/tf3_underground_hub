# Levels are plain two-track side platforms

Every level of the hub is a two-track platform with two side platforms, like layout 1 of Underground Railway Station. The other six layouts of that mod (island platforms, through tracks, four and six tracks) are not carried over, and the layout dropdown does not exist. A choice of layout per level would multiply the parameters and the vertical space of a stack, and the owner needs only side platforms. Bringing a layout back later means a new level model, because the level code assumes two tracks.
